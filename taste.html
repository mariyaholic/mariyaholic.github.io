How I like to work

Make room for judgment.

Not really rules. I like clear limits, tools that are nice to use, and work that holds up when you look twice.

Short version

Check it first, get excited after. Every interface needs a reason.

Three things

Rigour, taste, initiative.

These come from my preference notes, in plain words.

Rigour

Judge how you got there, not the headline. Show how strong the evidence is. If something isn't resolved, leave it unresolved.

Taste

Interfaces should feel thought through, work from the keyboard, and stay calm. Credentials stay private. Ambience stays optional.

Initiative

If the scope is clear, find the work that matters and do the smallest useful piece, without asking about every little thing.

The feeling underneath

Stubborn, mostly.

I keep at it because I want to make things that are true and worth coming back to.
