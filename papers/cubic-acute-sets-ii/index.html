Summary

Let a(n) be the largest size of a set S ⊆ {0,1}n in which every three distinct points form only strictly acute angles in ℝn (OEIS A089676). Part II extends the dependency-free Lean 4.35.0 library of Part I from 8 to 15 modules and from 267 to 386 public theorems. Every result below is checked by the Lean kernel.

  1. The exact value a(8) = 10. No 11-point acute set exists in {0,1}8. A binary-split bitset checker covers all 139 canonical root tuples.
  2. Harangi's Theorem 3.14 for s = 3, through a 2 × 5 Vandermonde matrix modulo a prime p ≥ 5, with refutations at p = 4 and p = 3.
  3. New acute sets for 18 ≤ n ≤ 26 of sizes 134, 137, 142, 149, 171, 204, 219, 238 and 250, from a conflict-guided search.
  4. Certified lower bounds for every 0 ≤ n ≤ 100. Against the OEIS table of 2 November 2026 they are higher at 28 dimensions, equal at 26 and lower at 47.

Upper bounds for a(9) = 16 and a(10) = 17, finite-field constructions over 𝔽pr (r ≥ 2) and compositions with s ≥ 4 are not yet formalized. Exact values for n ≥ 11 remain open.

This summary is a shortened form. The PDF has the full abstract and the provenance statement.
