Colophon

How this site is made.

Warm paper colours, Fraunces and Newsreader, one oxblood accent, and plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

One rule

Things can move, but everything has to read fine with motion off.

Sky/Hearth/Arcade/Companions/Tools/Taste

Design notes

Laid out like a magazine, not a dashboard.

The look comes from the approved combo draft: paper colours, open margins, wide images and oxblood rules. The other pages change up the layout a bit.

Type

Light: Fraunces for headings, Newsreader for body text, system sans for the UI. Night: Major Mono Display for headings, Special Elite for labels, Newsreader for body text.

Motion

Animations only use transform and opacity. You can pause them, and the site follows your reduced-motion setting.

Interaction

Links are real links. The project switcher on the home page uses real buttons with focus states. The mobile menu works with a keyboard.

Fallback

With JavaScript off, every page still has its text, links, images and reading order.

Sources and limits

Where the content comes from.

Project text comes from my persona drafts, the READMEs, the public sites and dated notes. Screenshots are local captures of Cygnus, Uika, TickAtlas, and Starfire concept art.

Rights gate
The portrait and character art are for private review only. Their sources don't allow public redistribution, so they need to be swapped for cleared art before publishing.

Approved direction

The combo draft gave the site its paper colours, Fraunces, the oxblood accent, wide images and light motion.

One system

Sky, Hearth, Emberkind, Companions, Tools, Atlas, Arcade, Taste and Colophon all use the same stylesheet and layout.

Still unverified

Not checked yet: Safari, Firefox, screen readers, real hosting, and publication rights.

Credits · every font, its licence and any change

Font credits.

All fonts are self-hosted from fonts/, with their licence texts next to them. The subsets count as modified versions and are labelled that way. OFL 1.1 allows that because none of these fonts has a Reserved Font Name, and Apache 2.0 §4 asks for the change notice.
