Colophon

How this site is made.

Sixteen colours, one bitmap font, dithered pictures, and plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

One rule

Things can move, but everything has to read fine with motion off.

Design notes

A desktop from about 1992.

The look borrows from NEC PC-98 software: a fixed palette, a checkered desktop, and every section in its own window. The home page is the desktop itself.

Type

Departure Mono for everything you read, JetBrains Mono for the text-mode star charts. Every picture is dithered to the same sixteen colours.

Motion

Animations step instead of glide, and they switch off when your system asks for reduced motion.

Interaction

Links are real links. Three in the header, two in the footer, and everything else in the index.

Fallback

With JavaScript off, every page still has its text, links, images and reading order.

Sources and limits

Where the content comes from.

Project text comes from my persona drafts, the READMEs, the public sites and dated notes. Screenshots are local captures of Cygnus, Uika, TickAtlas, and Starfire concept art.

Rights gate
The portrait and character art are for private review only. Their sources don't allow public redistribution, so they need to be swapped for cleared art before publishing.

Approved direction

The combo draft gave the site its paper colours, Fraunces, the oxblood accent, wide images and light motion.

One system

Every page uses the same stylesheet and layout.

Still unverified

Not checked yet: Safari, Firefox, screen readers, real hosting, and publication rights.
