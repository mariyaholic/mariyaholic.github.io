Astronomy · Ad Astra / Cygnus

The sky, on record.

I go back through public archives looking for signals the standard pipelines might have missed. Then I try to explain them away before calling anything a lead.

Ad Astra stores the patches, field images and Gaia stars, plus the record that links them. It reads from TESS, Kepler, Gaia, WISE, ZTF and the Legacy Surveys, each with source notes.

Cygnus is the part you can browse. Open leads stay unverified by definition, and retractions stay visible: sixty-five first leads were narrowed down, and the rejection notes are part of the result.
