Media plan

This page plans the screenshots and recordings for the project guides. These assets do not exist yet. The written instructions work without them.

Note

Capture only live application states or clearly labelled synthetic harnesses.

CSV Viewer and Parquet Viewer

Capture the opening of a synthetic file, the table, partial Find coverage and the inspector. Record a short clip of open, page, find, select and copy for each app separately.

Yozora

Capture the source switches, Apply for the priority list, and the Discord-offline state in the synthetic UI harness. Record a short keyboard-reorder clip beside the instructions.

MH Ticker

Capture the v1.2.3 strip and panel. Label images from a newer source build separately. Record a short clip that shows how to expand the panel, select a symbol and read the quote age.

Starfire Hearth

Capture the title screen, the first scenario, the objective and the debrief. Record a short clip of a legal action and its turn result from a fixed-seed run.

Omoi

Capture an empty synthetic catalogue, the search and the export controls. Record a short clip of search and filtering without real browser history.

Uika and Sitekit

Use text for commands. Record a short terminal session that shows a synthetic dry check, change and verification. Include the full transcript.

Ad Astra

Show a still of the public sky explorer and a public or synthetic evidence-labelled report. Record a short clip of selecting a target and opening its evidence panel.

Before publishing

  1. Record the source revision, platform, task and fixture for every asset.
  2. Remove personal information, tokens, notifications and private paths from every frame.
  3. Add alt text, a caption or a transcript, a still alternative and user-controlled playback.
  4. Check the captures again when the UI changes.
