Draft: privacy

Keep the line visible.

Be clear about what you can look at, what's been aggregated, and what stays private. If the line is written down, you can check it.

Display class

A draft policy. Nothing private is linked here: no files, credentials, raw rows or uncleared art.

Four display classes

Pick the class first.

Blurring something doesn't make it safe. Each item needs a class, a source note, and a check that it can't be traced back.

Publicidentity, mechanism, approved surface
Aggregatedranges, buckets, medians, or quantiles
Syntheticseeded example unrelated to source rows
Privateexact outcomes, identifiers, paths, or tokens

The ladder

What can go on a page.

Step through it, from public stuff down to what has to stay private.

Public

Project names, how things work at a high level, approved screenshots, rough page counts and design stage. Label things "local capture", "concept art" or "in development" where it fits.

Aggregated

Rough ranges, normalized indexes and grouped counts that can't point to one run, account, symbol, target or day. The grouping rule is recorded privately.

Synthetic/demo

Seeded toy data or a made-up trace. Put "Synthetic demonstration - not a measured result" in the caption and alt text.

Private

Exact financial metrics, raw trades, account or broker details, precise timestamps, local paths, credentials, private chat text, and live or promotion status. No public link.

Before a release

Check the crop.

A screenshot can show more than you meant. Browser bars, URLs, titles and text in the image all count. Nothing live, broker, public bind or promotion goes out without explicit sign-off.

Rights and re-identification

The owner decides.

NIST's release-model guidance helps you think it through. It doesn't clear anything. The owner still decides if the audience, date range and source can go out together.

Right now
The portrait and character art stay private until there's a cleared replacement or a written OK to publish.
