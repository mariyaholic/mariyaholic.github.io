The ladder

Walk the release boundary.

Public

Publish project names, high-level mechanics, approved screenshots, rough page counts, and design stage. Label captures as local capture, concept art, or in development.

Aggregated

Publish rough ranges, normalized indexes, and grouped counts that hide any single run, account, symbol, target, or day.

Synthetic/demo

Use seeded toy data or a synthetic trace. Put "Synthetic demonstration - not a measured result" in the caption and alt text.

Private

Hold back exact financial metrics, raw trades, account details, timestamps, local paths, credentials, private chat text, and live or promotion status.

Before a release

Check the crop.

Inspect browser bars, URLs, titles, and image text before release. Require explicit sign-off before publishing anything live, broker-linked, or promoted.
